# The reed-bed question: the full picture

Generated by `workflows/paper/p96b_wetland_evidence.py` from the publication tables of this build — do not edit by hand.
The question: how much of the June 2023 inundation was new flooding of dry ground, and what was the vegetated wetland
(reed beds of the Kherson delta and of the floodway between the dam and Kherson) before the breach. Three pre-event ground
classes (p95x `ground_class.tif`): dry before the event / seasonally wet vegetated wetland / open reference water.
